Vision and Ethos

Education at Shalom International School is about self-confidence, challenge, and enjoyment. It teaches the importance of constructive questioning and a sense of responsibility with compassion, confidence, and creativity.

Core Values

Our foundational pillars guide students toward balanced personal and intellectual growth:

  • Compassion: Fostering empathy, care, and a heart for service towards others.
  • Confidence: Building self-assurance to face global challenges and excel in academics.
  • Creativity: Encouraging out-of-the-box thinking and adaptation to modern technological advancements.

Our Ethos

We empower our students to be on a constant pursuit of knowledge by strengthening their intellectual capacities and seeking better ways to assimilate information. By enabling our students to understand changes in the fields of technology and science, we strive to develop a creative mind that searches for new ways of being and doing at every point of life.

Our Vision

We strive to develop a balanced emotional character and personality, leading to self-awareness, empathy, and strong social skills. We envision empowering students to contentedly pursue knowledge, strengthen their intellectual capacities, and discover advanced pathways of learning.

Our Motto

Our guiding motto is “Lord is My Shepherd”.

We guide our students to grow in love and understanding, to practice their faith, to realize their fullest academic potential, and to emerge as responsible citizens who are Christ-like in their service to others.
