A Place to Learn & Grow Together

Shalom International School offers a range of extracurricular activities, such as sports, music, and art which help students develop their interests and talents outside of the classroom.

Our school is known for personalized attention, extracurricular activities, discipline, and safety of students. Our Student-Faculty Ratio is 15 : 1 — we enroll a maximum of 25 students per class.

Our Motto

Our motto is "Lord is My Shepherd." We guide our students to grow in love, understanding, and to practice their faith, to realize their academic potential and to be responsible citizens who are Christ-like in their service to others. We believe that a successful school feels like home, and we work to develop a balanced emotional character, self-awareness, empathy and good social skills in every student.
