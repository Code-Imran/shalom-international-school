Parents Declairation

Parents and guardians are requested to note the following regarding additional expenses during the academic year:

  • If your ward requires extra coaching or additional tuition, the applicable fees will be payable by you.
  • Any additional expenses incurred by the school for your child/ward, such as medical expenses, birthday celebrations, activities, or other necessary requirements, will be payable by you as applicable.
  • Such expenses will be communicated by the Accounts Department of Shalom International School & Jr. College, Panchgani, and are expected to be settled accordingly.

Please review these guidelines carefully before proceeding with the admission process.

Important Note on Vacations & Outings: Kindly go through the school annual fixture before planning any vacations or outings. Students will not be allowed to be taken away from the school outside of the exact dates mentioned in the school calendar.
