Student Requirement List

Parents are requested to prepare their wards with the essential items listed below before reporting to the residential campus:

Clothing & Wears

  • 3 pairs of color cloths
  • 6 pairs of inner-wears
  • 2 night suits

Socks & Footwear

  • 6 pairs of navy blue socks
  • 3 pairs of color socks
  • 1 pair of school shoes
  • 1 pair of sport shoes
  • 1 pair of floaters

Personal Essentials & Linens

  • 1 toiletry pouch
  • 2 bath towels

Mandatory Admission Documents Required

Please ensure you bring clear copies and original documents during the admission and reporting process:

  1. Two passport size photos (colour) of the student along with a copy of their Aadhar card.
  2. One passport size photo of each parent (mother/father) along with copies of their Aadhar cards.
  3. One Xerox copy of the Birth Certificate.
  4. One Xerox copy of the Annual Report Card.
  5. Original School Leaving Certificate (countersigned).

Important Note on Vacations & Outings: Kindly go through the school annual fixture before planning any vacations or outings. Students will not be allowed to be taken away from the school outside of the exact dates mentioned in the school calendar.
