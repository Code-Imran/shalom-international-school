Visionary Leadership & Administration

The success, values, and forward-looking vision of Shalom International School & Jr. College are guided by a dedicated leadership team committed to fostering holistic growth, moral integrity, discipline, and academic excellence.

Mr. Sahebrao Shivram Biramane

Founder (Ex-President, Panchgani Municipal Corporation)

Founder of Saikrupa Charitable Trust & Saikrupa Education Foundation. A well-known philanthropist who runs charitable institutions and schools dedicated to community upliftment and empowering children through accessible, high-quality education in the Panchgani region.

Mr. Ajit Biramane

Chairman

His management style is dialogue-oriented and result-driven. His progressive approach has helped the institution upgrade digital classrooms, sports complexes, and hostel amenities. Deeply committed to student excellence in both academics and athletics, under his dynamic chairmanship the school continues to reach new educational heights.

Mrs. Shweta Ajit Biramane

Administrator

The compassionate, student-first approach of the Administrator creates a nurturing environment for boarders and day scholars alike. Children experience warmth and constant care under her supervision, making her the driving force behind the seamless pastoral care and daily operations of the school.
