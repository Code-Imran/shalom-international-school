Affiliation, Curriculum and School Calendar

The School follows the syllabus prescribed by the Indian Council of Secondary Education, New Delhi (CISCE Board) and Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education (S.S.C. & H.S.C Board) and prepares its pupils from Kindergarten to the Secondary School Examination (SSC) X & Higher Secondary. Our Jr. College (H.S.C) XI and XII (Science and Commerce) follows the Syllabus laid down by the Government of Maharashtra. The School is also a member of the Indian Public Schools Conference.

Curriculum Overview

The general course of studies (Grades I to X) includes: English, Mathematics, Hindi, Marathi, History, Geography, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Applications, Commercial Application, Moral Science, Art, G.K., and SUPW. For Grades XI & XII Science and Commerce, the curriculum includes Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Information Technology, and English.

School Academic Sessions & Terms

One academic year consists of three dedicated terms designed for thorough coverage and holistic learning:

  • First term: June to August
  • Second term: September to November
  • Third term: January to April

All students are expected to go home during long vacations. The School fixtures, sports timetables, and academic schedules will be strictly followed.

Co-Curricular & Sports Activities

Co-curricular activities are an integral part of the training given in school. They include Dramatics, Debating, Elocution, Eisteddfod, Dancing, Drawing, Painting, Art & Craft, Quizzes, and Olympiads.

The school provides a number of indoor and outdoor games under the guidance and supervision of trained staff. We have facilities for professional coaching in Football, Cricket, Volleyball, Athletics, Gymnastics, and Skating. Games are an integral part of the school curriculum, and no student is exempted from games. Indoor options include Table Tennis, Badminton, Chess, Carrom, and Pool—especially enjoyed during the monsoon season.

Science & IT Labs

To enable children to keep pace with the ever-growing world of technology, the school has a well-organized computer laboratory with high-speed internet connections, where each child is provided a separate computer to work on. We offer cutting-edge training at the Junior College level with balanced preparation for various entrance exams (JEE, NEET, MH-CET). The campus facilities and guidance of qualified professionals provide a unique environment for competitive learning.
