State-of-the-Art Infrastructure & Student Amenities

Our school campus offers a world-class learning experience with the latest classroom facilities and infrastructure designed to foster a secure, nurturing environment for all students.

Hostel Accommodation

Laced with modern amenities, two separate but twin hostel buildings for girls and boys enhance the beauty of the campus. They provide a safe, home-away-from-home atmosphere under the careful supervision of resident wardens and staff.

Games & Sports

To help children build a healthy and strong physique, a well-equipped gymnasium plays a vital role in building muscles and developing strong bones. Riding and skating have also been recently introduced much to the delight of the students. Professional coaching is provided for a variety of outdoor and indoor games.

Library & Laboratories

To promote the reading habit of the child and to keep them up to date with the latest knowledge, the school features a rich collection of books on all subjects suitable for all age groups. Separate laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, and Biology are fully equipped for practical learning.

Infirmary

A well-trained staff nurse is available on the school premises 24 hours a day to ensure the health and well-being of pupils. Emergencies are promptly treated at the hospitals in Wai, and medicines prescribed by doctors are administered to the child strictly by the matron or hostel in-charge.

Food & Dining

A sound mind dwells in a sound body. To ensure physical growth and overall wellness, well-balanced, nutritious, wholesome, tasty, and homely food is served across all meals.

Information Technology

To enable children to pace with the ever-growing world of technology, the school has a well-organized computer laboratory with an internet connection, where each child is provided a separate computer to work on.
